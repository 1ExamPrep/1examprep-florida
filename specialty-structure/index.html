Who needs the Florida Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties license?

The Florida Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties license covers screen enclosures, aluminum structures and the structural carpentry, concrete, masonry and steel specialties. Most buyers are screen enclosure, aluminum and structural trade contractors. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties exam?

Division II candidates pass the Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.
