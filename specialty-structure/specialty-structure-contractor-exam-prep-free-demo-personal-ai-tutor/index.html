Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties license covers screen enclosures, aluminum structures and the structural carpentry, concrete, masonry and steel specialties. Division II candidates pass the Specialty Structure and Structural Specialties trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
