Who needs the Florida Class B Air Conditioning license?

The Florida Class B Air Conditioning license covers air conditioning and heating up to 25 tons of cooling and 500,000 BTU of heat. Most buyers are residential and light commercial HVAC contractors. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Class B Air Conditioning exam?

Division II candidates pass the Class B Air Conditioning trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Florida Class B Air Conditioning license?

What exams do I take for Class B Air Conditioning?

Division II candidates pass the Class B Air Conditioning trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for Class B Air Conditioning?

How do I schedule the exam?

Register with Professional Testing, Inc. and get approved, then book your seats with Pearson VUE. You can test before you apply for the license itself.

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
