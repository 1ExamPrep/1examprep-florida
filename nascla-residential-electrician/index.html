Who needs the Florida NASCLA Residential Electrical license?

The Florida NASCLA Residential Electrical license uses the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Residential Electrical Contractors; confirm with the ECLB that it fits your application before you book. Most buyers are residential electricians who work in more than one state. The state certification lets you contract statewide, while counties still issue journeyman cards.

What is on the NASCLA Residential Electrical exam?

ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.
