What does the GITS Master Plumber - PAE1B include?

The GITS Master Plumber - PAE1B examination, administered by Governmental Inspection, Testing, and Services (GITS), is a county-level licensing requirement in Florida for plumbing professionals authorized to plan, install, maintain, and supervise plumbing systems for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Journeyman and Master Plumber license is issued by counties, which test on the plumbing code through Prov or GITS. County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.

How should you study with it?

Read the book once for structure, then use it for lookups during practice exams. Mark the pages you return to most with tabs. The licensing guide lays out the order of the steps, from registering with PTI to booking Pearson VUE.
