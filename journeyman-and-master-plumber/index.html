Who needs the Florida Journeyman and Master Plumber license?

The Florida Journeyman and Master Plumber license is issued by counties, which test on the plumbing code through Prov or GITS. Most buyers are plumbers testing for a county card. A county card is good only where that county or city recognizes it, so check with your local licensing office.

What is on the Journeyman and Master Plumber exam?

County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.
