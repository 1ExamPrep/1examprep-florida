Who needs the Florida Modular Plans Examiner license?

The Florida Modular Plans Examiner license covers reviewing plans for factory-built modular buildings. Most buyers are modular plan reviewers. Florida licenses inspectors and plans examiners through DBPR, and local building departments hire from that list.

What is on the Modular Plans Examiner exam?

Florida inspector and plans examiner candidates pass an ICC technical exam and the Florida Principles and Practice exam, which Pearson VUE schedules after DBPR authorizes you.
