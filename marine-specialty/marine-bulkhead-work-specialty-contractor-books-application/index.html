Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Marine Specialty license covers docks, seawalls, bulkheads, piles and other marine structures, with separate bulkhead, dock, pile and seawall options. Division II candidates pass the Marine Specialty trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
