How should you read exam prep reviews?

A 5-star review from someone who took a different exam tells you less than a 4-star review from someone who took yours. Use the table to weigh what you read.

Frequently asked questions

Are these reviews real?

Yes. The Google reviews come straight from the 1 Exam Prep Google business profile, and Shopper Approved only surveys people who placed an order. 1 Exam Prep does not write, edit or pick them.

Why do the reviews mention other states and trades?

1 Exam Prep sells exam prep for contractor, electrical, plumbing and HVAC licenses across the country, and one store fills every order. The service, shipping and books are the same for the Florida contractor exam.

Can 1 Exam Prep remove a bad review?

No. Google removes a review only when it breaks Google's content policy, and Shopper Approved does not let merchants delete ratings. The team can reply publicly and try to fix the problem.

No. The reviews load on this page for anyone. You need a Google account only to write a Google review.

How long do books take to arrive?

Books ship free by USPS Media Mail inside the United States, which usually takes 2 to 8 business days. Online courses arrive by email after checkout. Allow up to 15 business days for rental, Ultimate and 1 Package orders.
