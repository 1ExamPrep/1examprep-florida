Who needs the Florida Solar Contractor license?

The Florida Solar Contractor license covers solar energy systems, including solar water heating and photovoltaic installations. Most buyers are solar installers who want to qualify their own company. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Solar Contractor exam?

Division II candidates pass the Solar Contractor trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Florida Solar Contractor license?

The Florida Solar Contractor license covers solar energy systems, including solar water heating and photovoltaic installations. Most buyers are solar installers who want to qualify their own company. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What exams do I take for Solar Contractor?

Division II candidates pass the Solar Contractor trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for Solar Contractor?

The lowest priced item here is Florida Solar Contractor Exam - Online Practice Questions at $79.99. Most candidates pair the books with a course.

How do I schedule the exam?

Register with Professional Testing, Inc. and get approved, then book your seats with Pearson VUE. You can test before you apply for the license itself.

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
