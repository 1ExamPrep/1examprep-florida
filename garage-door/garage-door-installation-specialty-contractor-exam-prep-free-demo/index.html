Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Garage Door Installation Specialty license covers installing and repairing garage doors and their openers, including wind-load ratings. Division II candidates pass the Garage Door Installation Specialty trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
