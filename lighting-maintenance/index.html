Who needs the Florida Lighting Maintenance Specialty license?

The Florida Lighting Maintenance Specialty license covers maintaining and repairing lighting fixtures and their ballasts and lamps. Most buyers are lighting service companies. The state certification lets you contract statewide, while counties still issue journeyman cards.

What is on the Lighting Maintenance Specialty exam?

ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.
