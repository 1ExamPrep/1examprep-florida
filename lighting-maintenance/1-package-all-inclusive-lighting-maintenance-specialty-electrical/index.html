Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Lighting Maintenance Specialty license covers maintaining and repairing lighting fixtures and their ballasts and lamps. ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.

How should you study with it?

Plan on 8 to 10 weeks. Start the DBPR application in week 1, study Business and Finance in weeks 1 to 3, then take one trade topic a week and finish with 2 timed practice runs. The licensing guide lays out the order of the steps, from registering with PTI to booking Pearson VUE.
