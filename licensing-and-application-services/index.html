Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Florida Licensing and Application Services license?

The Florida Licensing and Application Services license covers help with the DBPR application, exam registration, endorsement, qualifying a second business and forming your company. Most buyers are applicants who want the paperwork handled for them.

What exams do I take for Licensing and Application Services?

The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for Licensing and Application Services?

The lowest priced item here is Florida State Exam Registration - Construction Industry Licensing Board at $79. Most candidates pair the books with a course.

Who has to pass Business and Finance?

Every certified construction contractor passes it once, whatever the trade. Electrical contractors take the ECLB business exam instead, and a current CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Can I buy a single book instead of a package?

Yes. Single references, tabs and the Florida Contractors Manual are sold on their own, so you can fill gaps in a library you already own.
