Who needs the Florida Underground Utility and Excavation license?

The Florida Underground Utility and Excavation license covers main sanitary sewer, storm, water and other underground utility lines and the excavation for them. Most buyers are site work and utility contractors. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Underground Utility and Excavation exam?

Division II candidates pass the Underground Utility and Excavation trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

How do I schedule the exam?

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
