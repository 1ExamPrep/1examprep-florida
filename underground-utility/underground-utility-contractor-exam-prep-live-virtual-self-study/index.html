Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Underground Utility and Excavation license covers main sanitary sewer, storm, water and other underground utility lines and the excavation for them. Division II candidates pass the Underground Utility and Excavation trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
