Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Building Contractor license covers commercial buildings and single- or multiple-family homes up to 3 stories tall. Division I candidates pass 3 parts: Contract Administration, Project Management and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
