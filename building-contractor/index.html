Who needs the Florida Building Contractor license?

The Florida Building Contractor license covers commercial buildings and single- or multiple-family homes up to 3 stories tall. Most buyers are commercial builders who stay at 3 stories or under. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Building Contractor exam?

Division I candidates pass 3 parts: Contract Administration, Project Management and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Florida Building Contractor license?

The Florida Building Contractor license covers commercial buildings and single- or multiple-family homes up to 3 stories tall. Most buyers are commercial builders who stay at 3 stories or under. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What exams do I take for Building Contractor?

Division I candidates pass 3 parts: Contract Administration, Project Management and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for Building Contractor?

The lowest priced item here is Florida Building Contractor Exam Prep Study Flash Cards at $75. Most candidates pair the books with a course.

How do I schedule the exam?

Register with Professional Testing, Inc. and get approved, then book your seats with Pearson VUE. You can test before you apply for the license itself.

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
