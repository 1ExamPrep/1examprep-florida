What should you include in your message?

A few details let the team recommend the right package on the first reply instead of the third. This site covers 61 Florida licenses, and the approved books differ for each one, so the license matters most of all.

  • Your license, such as General Contractor, Air A or Unlimited Electrical
  • Your Pearson VUE test date, or the month you hope to test
  • Which approved books you already own
  • Whether you still need Business and Finance or the DBPR application
