Who needs the Florida Residential Mechanical Inspector license?

The Florida Residential Mechanical Inspector license covers residential mechanical inspection. Most buyers are mechanical inspectors. Florida licenses inspectors and plans examiners through DBPR, and local building departments hire from that list.

What is on the Residential Mechanical Inspector exam?

Florida inspector and plans examiner candidates pass an ICC technical exam and the Florida Principles and Practice exam, which Pearson VUE schedules after DBPR authorizes you.
