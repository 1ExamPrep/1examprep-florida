Who needs the Florida Residential Pool/Spa Contractor license?

The Florida Residential Pool/Spa Contractor license covers swimming pools and spas at one-, two- and three-family homes. Most buyers are residential pool builders. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Residential Pool/Spa Contractor exam?

Division II candidates pass the Residential Pool/Spa Contractor trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.
