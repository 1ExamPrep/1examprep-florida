Who needs the Florida Residential Electrical Contractor license?

The Florida Residential Electrical Contractor license covers electrical work on one- and two-family homes and similar residential buildings. Most buyers are residential electricians starting their own company. The state certification lets you contract statewide, while counties still issue journeyman cards.

What is on the Residential Electrical Contractor exam?

ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.
