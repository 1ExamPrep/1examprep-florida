Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Journeyman Electrician license is issued by Florida counties and cities, not the state, and is usually tested by Prov or GITS on the National Electrical Code. County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.

How should you study with it?

Read the book once for structure, then use it for lookups during practice exams. Mark the pages you return to most with tabs. The licensing guide lays out the order of the steps, from registering with PTI to booking Pearson VUE.
