Who needs the Florida Journeyman Electrician license?

The Florida Journeyman Electrician license is issued by Florida counties and cities, not the state, and is usually tested by Prov or GITS on the National Electrical Code. Most buyers are electricians who work under a licensed contractor. A county card is good only where that county or city recognizes it, so check with your local licensing office.

What is on the Journeyman Electrician exam?

County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.
