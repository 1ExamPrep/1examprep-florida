Who needs the Florida Glass and Glazing Specialty license?

The Florida Glass and Glazing Specialty license covers storefronts, curtain wall, windows and other glass and glazing systems. Most buyers are glazing contractors. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the Glass and Glazing Specialty exam?

Division II candidates pass the Glass and Glazing Specialty trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Florida Glass and Glazing Specialty license?

The Florida Glass and Glazing Specialty license covers storefronts, curtain wall, windows and other glass and glazing systems. Most buyers are glazing contractors. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What exams do I take for Glass and Glazing Specialty?

Division II candidates pass the Glass and Glazing Specialty trade knowledge exam and Business and Finance. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for Glass and Glazing Specialty?

How do I schedule the exam?

Register with Professional Testing, Inc. and get approved, then book your seats with Pearson VUE. You can test before you apply for the license itself.

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
