Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Master Electrician license is a county license for electricians who supervise work, tested on the National Electrical Code. County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.

How should you study with it?

Spend 30 to 60 minutes a day for 4 to 6 weeks. Keep the approved books open while you practice so the course trains your lookups. The licensing guide lays out the order of the steps, from registering with PTI to booking Pearson VUE.
