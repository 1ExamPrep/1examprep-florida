Who needs the Florida Master Electrician license?

The Florida Master Electrician license is a county license for electricians who supervise work, tested on the National Electrical Code. Most buyers are electricians qualifying for a county master or contractor card. A county card is good only where that county or city recognizes it, so check with your local licensing office.

What is on the Master Electrician exam?

County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.
