Who needs the Florida NASCLA Commercial General Building license?

The Florida NASCLA Commercial General Building license uses the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors, which the CILB has accepted in place of the Florida trade exam since March 1, 2019. Most buyers are builders licensed in other NASCLA states, or who plan to be. A state certified license lets you work anywhere in Florida, and it is issued to you as the qualifier of your business.

What is on the NASCLA Commercial General Building exam?

Florida accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building score in place of its own trade exam. You still pass Florida Business and Finance and a Florida Building Code portion.

Who needs the Florida NASCLA Commercial General Building license?

What exams do I take for NASCLA Commercial General Building?

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for NASCLA Commercial General Building?

How do I schedule the exam?

Register with Professional Testing, Inc. and get approved, then book your seats with Pearson VUE. You can test before you apply for the license itself.

Do I have to take Business and Finance again for a second license?

No. A current, active CILB licensee who tests for a new category is exempt from Business and Finance under rule 61G4-16.0015.
