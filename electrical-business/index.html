Who needs the Florida Electrical Contractor Business Exam license?

The Florida Electrical Contractor Business Exam license is the business part of every ECLB certification, built on the Florida Contractors Manual. Most buyers are every ECLB applicant. The state certification lets you contract statewide, while counties still issue journeyman cards.

What is on the Electrical Contractor Business Exam exam?

ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.
