Who needs the Florida Unlimited Electrical Contractor license?

The Florida Unlimited Electrical Contractor license covers all electrical work with no limit on building type or size. Most buyers are electrical contractors who want to qualify a company statewide. The state certification lets you contract statewide, while counties still issue journeyman cards.

What is on the Unlimited Electrical Contractor exam?

ECLB certification exams come in 2 parts, Technical and Safety and Business, both taken at Pearson VUE. The Technical and Safety part has 100 questions in 5.5 hours, and the Business part has 50.

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.
