Who needs the Florida County Exams by GITS license?

The Florida County Exams by GITS license covers the county certificates of competency that GITS tests, such as the Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade contractor categories. Most buyers are contractors who only need a county certificate. A county card is good only where that county or city recognizes it, so check with your local licensing office.

What is on the County Exams by GITS exam?

County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.

Who needs the Florida County Exams by GITS license?

The Florida County Exams by GITS license covers the county certificates of competency that GITS tests, such as the Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade contractor categories. Most buyers are contractors who only need a county certificate. A county card is good only where that county or city recognizes it, so check with your local licensing office.

What exams do I take for County Exams by GITS?

County exams are set by each county board and given by Prov or GITS, so the book list follows your county's bulletin. Many counties also require a Business and Law exam.

Is the Florida contractor exam open book?

Yes. The state construction and electrical exams are open book, but only the references in the Candidate Information Booklet are allowed, so tabbed and highlighted books save time.
