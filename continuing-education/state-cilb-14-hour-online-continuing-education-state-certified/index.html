What does the Florida State CILB - 14 Hour Online Continuing Education include?

The Florida State Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB) requires certified and registered contractors to complete 14 hours of continuing education every two years to maintain active licensure.

  • Online, self-paced
  • Certificate on completion
  • Reported as the course describes

Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Continuing Education license covers the 14-hour CILB renewal course and short pool and financial responsibility courses. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.

How should you study with it?

Finish the course before your renewal deadline and keep the certificate with your renewal records. The licensing guide lays out the order of the steps, from registering with PTI to booking Pearson VUE.
