Which Florida exam is it for?

The Florida Business and Finance license is the business exam every certified construction contractor passes, built on the Florida Contractors Manual. The Florida Business and Finance exam has 120 questions over about 6.5 hours, open book, and you need 70% to pass. Every certified construction contractor takes it once; a current, active CILB licensee adding a category is exempt.
